Good collaboration leaves a visible trail.

A shared project works best when decisions, responsibilities, and changes are easy for every team member to see and discuss.

Team rhythm

A lightweight way to stay aligned

  1. Frame

    Agree on the question, audience, and smallest useful outcome before dividing tasks.

  2. Assign

    Give each task one clear owner while keeping design and technical decisions open to review.

  3. Integrate

    Combine small changes frequently so the website remains usable throughout development.

  4. Reflect

    Record what changed, why it changed, and what the next person needs to know.

My contribution

Make the shared work easier to build on

I aim to contribute code that is readable, tested, and connected to the project’s purpose. That includes communicating blockers early, asking for review, and documenting any external data or AI assistance used in the work.

As the group project develops, this page can be updated with agreed roles, milestones, and evidence of the team’s process without exposing private contact details.
